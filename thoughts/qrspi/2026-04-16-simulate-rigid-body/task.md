The hp-sim-3d simulation should treat the slideprinter hexagon effector as a genuinely rigid assembly instead of as a soft cluster of pairwise distance constraints. The goal is to understand and likely refactor how the existing rigid-group, cable-constraint, orientation, velocity, and inertia machinery interact so the effector behaves like a single body and avoids sponge-like deformation and explosive correction events, informed by PBDBodies' single-body XPBD framing.
