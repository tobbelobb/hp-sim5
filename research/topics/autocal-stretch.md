# Autocal stretch and identifiability

The [HP3 investigation](../output/autocal_research_with_Fable5p2/report.md) shows that smooth elastic bias can hide in anchors despite tiny fit residuals. Its geometry substitution test identifies stretch in those simulations. Height diversity alone and more solver iterations alone did not solve the bias; constant weighted per-strand force plus an offset gave promising translation-aligned shape accuracy with an independent fitter. Absolute home shift remains. Production autocal integration, held-out validation and hardware transfer remain open. This does not supersede earlier collector/settling verification: those studies test different behavior.

The [production integration review](../output/program-2026-11-15-review-and-harden-stretch-insensitive-autocal-robust-bootstrap-h-3fa9855a/session-2026-11-15-review-production-integration-of-pose-fit-information-planning-a-84abd4be/report.md) identifies a sweep-profile lookup collision and unvalidated projection/spool combinations. Synthetic four-high and cube examples show that HP3's self-stress reachability and two-fixed-sphere curve do not generalize directly. These results do not invalidate the single HP3 d2 run, nor establish hardware behavior.
The [final home-shift investigation](../output/program-2026-11-15-review-and-harden-stretch-insensitive-autocal-robust-bootstrap-h-3fa9855a/session-2026-11-15-determine-and-implement-physically-justified-final-home-shift-co-fc8f5233/report.md) finds that fitted offsets and nominal firmware stiffness do not identify absolute home. RRF and Klipper already include an absolute negative flex term in their geometric-origin-referenced transform. An opt-in final correction requires independently established extensions in the actual reference states, a practically slack home, and identifiable geometry; current collection does not supply those inputs. A synthetic counterexample shows that the consistency check cannot detect every systematic extension error. Physics and hardware validation remain open.
A [current-HEAD production-path battery](../output/program-2026-11-15-review-and-harden-stretch-insensitive-autocal-robust-bootstrap-h-3fa9855a/session-2026-11-15-build-and-exercise-reproducible-full-hp3-hp4-autocal-sanity-batt-fc7253a7/report.md) reproduces the nominal HP3 result but finds 9.98 mm worst coordinate error (9.59 mm translation-aligned) in one simulated HP3 trial at half the nominal cable stiffness, despite quality score 1.36. The baked scene confirms the stiffness change. This contradicts any general robustness claim from the earlier independent-fitter experiments, but different collected sweeps and mechanics prevent attributing the full difference solely to elasticity. Hardware, noise, repeats, and matched-data tests remain open.
