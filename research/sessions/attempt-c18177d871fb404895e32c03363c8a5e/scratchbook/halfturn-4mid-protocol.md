# Half-turn plus lower sensor-force development trial

Combine the frozen half-turn endpoint retreat with the previously studied 4mid collection force instead of 5mid. The sensor is commanded to four times the automatically tuned mid force, capped by the existing maximum; commanded force is not measured tension. All other policies, known nominal radii, automatic stopping and final application remain stock. Run fresh 6301 after the 7301 half-turn experiment, sequentially, with finite 8M-step and 2400-second limits. Compare per-anchor applied errors, full terminal status, movement and residuals with matched 6301 stock and half-turn runs. Treat as development because its force policy was previously investigated on other worlds and 6301 has been examined. Do not modify in response to independent 7301 results. No plant truth enters selection or movement. If this combination looks promising, freeze it before separate varied-layout/friction evaluation.
