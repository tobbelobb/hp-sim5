# Development protocol: longer collection

Freeze before physics: compare stock acceptance to an experimental policy that suppresses ordinary accept decisions before iteration 9, then restores stock acceptance. Collection, planner, fitter, 30 mm known radii and automatic final application remain unchanged. Max 12 iterations and 8 million physics steps per run. Run stock then extended sequentially in independently initialized worlds with identical uneven friction and noise seed 6301. Random streams will not remain paired after trajectories diverge. Treat this as one development comparison, not independent validation. Compare actual automatic application, every anchor, failure/stop reason, physical work and baked settings. If extended fails to improve maximum and all-anchor errors, do not select it for validation. If it improves, freeze the policy and use unseen worlds and layouts for independent evaluation. No ground truth enters collection, stopping or choice of fit; truth evaluates final applied result. Overall session cap is 10 physical starts and 80 million steps, extendable after reviewing results. Explicit per-job step caps and normal process cleanup apply.
