Improve encoder-only HP3 autocalibration across physically different machines.
Updated 2026-11-06 after the human prioritized sub-millimetre accuracy, scaling and robustness across machines.

The goal is accurate, reliable anchors with less collection effort, preserving
one-click operation and automatic stopping. Use known spool radii, zero buildup
and disabled simulator line layering. Use HP3, not HP4. Real calibration has no
ground-truth anchors. Truth may construct and evaluate a simulated plant, but
must not choose measurements, fit seeds, thresholds or the applied answer.

The historical reference is output/extended-autocal/no-buildup-reference-003.
Also use a fresh current-code baseline for any live comparison. Physics, motor,
collector and fitting changes since that reference can change its results.

Human priorities and success criteria

Prioritize correctness and robustness across physical setups and investigate both
practical improvements and their causes. Aim for less than 1 mm Euclidean error
on every anchor in every simulated trial. More than 20 mm on any anchor is a
practical failure; the earlier benchmark was less than 5 mm on every anchor.
Report all per-anchor errors
and their maximum; RMSE alone is insufficient. Emphasize uneven motor friction,
different anchor layouts and simulated sensor noise. Collection time is secondary
to obtaining reliable results; there is no 20% increase cap. A small simulation
matrix cannot establish hardware reliability.

What earlier runs have already established

- The 2026-11-06 program distinguished an exact scale gauge from weak
  conditioning in an idealized model. Known 30 mm radii made the tested wide
  geometry locally identifiable; an unknown common radius retained an exact
  scale symmetry. Narrow motion weakened the smallest projected singular value
  from 0.0371 to 0.000923. This is not proof of production or global
  identifiability. A local production-objective Jacobian on one recorded world
  was full rank; its weakest direction was not mainly uniform scale. See
  research/output/program-4cfc6d5bf6a2484f976521ab8a321e92/session-attempt-c18177d871fb404895e32c03363c8a5e/report.md.
- The same program tested more collection, wider spacing, lower force,
  half-turn retreat, point filtering, robust fitting and zero-tension
  projection. None achieved every anchor below 1 mm across trials. Half-turn
  retreat reduced the worst error in two worlds but a zero-injected-noise
  trial failed while an encoder continued moving. In one 6301 world,
  projection with a longer settling deadline increased maximum applied error
  from 8.103 to 16.337 mm. The projection/noise-mean selection bug was fixed
  in production by commit 678f7659; this does not establish an accuracy gain.
  Avoid repeating these exact interventions without a new discriminator.
  See research/output/program-4cfc6d5bf6a2484f976521ab8a321e92/summary.html.
- A subsequent point-specific fixed-encoder ellipse fit changed applied errors
  on one matched 9301 world from 5.924/1.672/0.810/1.118 mm to
  4.536/2.014/0.763/3.797 mm. The maximum improved but B and D worsened;
  this is not a validated production improvement. An idealized example with
  large fixed-axis motion is not evidence that such motion is physically
  feasible. See research/output/program-4cfc6d5bf6a2484f976521ab8a321e92/session-attempt-8c3c74b7641e45c2acd1cd976f572e08/report.md.
- Turning off injected Gaussian encoder noise left the stock automatic
  workflow with up to 3.678 mm applied error in one world. Stretch, motor
  behaviour, vibration, quantization and other modeled effects remained, and
  automatic force tuning changed. This does not isolate any cause. The
  simulator's physics model and autocal's ideal geometric fitting model are
  distinct: a residual against the latter does not by itself prove a defect
  in either. No production end-to-end numerical precision audit, true-anchor
  residual breakdown or instrumented projection trace was completed.
- Collection profiling is complete enough to guide a new experiment. Settling,
  pull-outs, force tuning and travel all matter. Native Rust, headless collection
  and cheaper recording modes are available. Do not start another general
  profiling or engine-rewrite project without identifying a new bottleneck.
  See research/autocal-recording-performance.md and
  research/output/session-2026-10-15-native-optimization/abstract.md.
- Earlier warm-start work improved simulated HP3 fits without new measurements.
  Blind point thinning, more restarts and simply collecting at half density did
  not reliably preserve accuracy. Offline thinning and fresh coarse collection
  are different interventions. See
  research/output/session-2026-10-15-improve-encoder-based-cdpr-autocalibration-in-hp-sim5-the-curren-98170696/report.md.
- A distinct-fit consensus-spread stopping rule has been investigated and
  implemented. Agreement can still reflect two biased fits. A new stopping rule
  needs evidence beyond remeasuring convergence on the same nominal world. See
  research/output/session-2026-10-15-improve-encoder-based-cdpr-autocalibration-in-hp-sim5-the-curren-b75b3bdf/report.md.
- It is no longer true that every experiment used one physical setup. A paired
  ten-world study varied mover mass 0.80-1.25x and gravity 8.5-11.0 m/s^2, keeping
  anchor layout, friction and stiffness fixed. Mean applied anchor RMSE was
  2.967 mm for L-BFGS-B and 2.874 mm for TRF. Neither solver had a candidate
  above 20 mm. These mild load changes do not establish robustness to large
  failures or other machine properties. See section 10 of
  research/output/session-2026-10-15-improve-encoder-based-cdpr-autocalibration-in-hp-sim5-the-curren-9d784051/report.md.
- TRF is available, but a global solver switch and per-restart fallback were
  rejected after legacy-data regressions. Better intermediate fits can create a
  tight biased consensus and worsen the final answer. Keep final applied error,
  per-iteration errors and extreme failures separate. The same report's sections
  9-11 document the distinction. Disagreement-driven fresh collection remains
  proposed, not demonstrated.
- Force sensitivity is measured on the nominal world and is not monotonic.
  A lower sensor force helped some runs but cost time; no robust force policy
  across different line properties or layouts was established. See sections
  4.4 and 4.7 of that report. Do not claim that weaker forces always help.
- Moving the encoder reference toward the anchor centroid has also been tested.
  Fresh centered collection slightly improved final RMSE, 4.588 to 4.277 mm,
  while increasing points from 140 to 180. An asymmetric reference was worse.
  Retrospective rebasing changes reference/noise assumptions and is not an
  equivalent substitute for fresh collection. See
  research/output/session-2026-10-20-interactive-research-ae0174ce/report.md.
- Generic ranking and finite-prediction-only consensus membership have mixed
  results. Validation must use compatible whole sweeps unseen by every member
  of the actual applied anchor-plus-spool model. See relevant earlier reports in research/catalog.md
  and research/output/session-2026-10-23-consensus-membership/report.md.

- The 2026-11-05 guide-friction study tested a bounded preload change in an
  isolated harness. It preserved nominal accuracy but did not recover collection.
  Three completed calibrations had all anchors below 5 mm; four active-guide
  trials failed before fitting. A matched zero-guide/noise-only control passed.
  A constructed probe found load torque with macroscopic local cable slack.
  The independent reviewer kept the scoped report-only result. No production
  change was adopted, and hardware robustness remains unresolved.
  The original workspace files were recovered after an execution-attachment
  failure. The full original evidence and raw journals are committed in
  the research/autocal-originals-2026-11-05-0a82df7f and research/autocal-recovery-2026-11-05-0a82df7f branches.
  Do not repeat the same preload/guide matrix as an untested improvement.

- The 2026-11-05 fixed lower-force matrix is complete: six primary attempts in three frozen HP3 worlds. Development maximum applied error improved 3.6959 to 2.8529 mm at 1.0641 times simulated effort. Evaluation improved 3.4116 to 2.7518 mm at 1.0709 times effort, with B worsening 1.1409 to 2.4098 mm. All five completed calibrations had every anchor below 5 mm. The nominal 4mid workflow failed before fitting on a 5000 ms M569.3 transport timeout, so the no-new-failure gate rejects force-default adoption. Keep 5mid. One invalid import-path startup is preserved separately and counted against the seven-collection allocation. Do not repeat this exact fixed4 matrix or the rejected preload matrix. A separate real-WebSocket discriminator reproduced an explicit 120-second encoder budget being capped at 5 seconds. The narrow transport candidate accepts the same valid six-second reply while preserving the effective 5-second default, shorter deadlines and bounded missing/disconnected failures. It passed 45 JavaScript and two noncollection headless checks. No full-autocal-with-fix or hardware reliability claim is made. See research/output/program-0a82df7fd4b34b8181716630492a1af4/lower-force/report.md.

Remaining promising questions

Priority: why does the production workflow miss the true anchors?

Treat numerical errors, weak conditioning, physical-model mismatch and
optimizer/selection behaviour as competing explanations. Do not assume the
simulator is wrong or that a zero-noise run has removed physical imperfections.
The following are proposed experiments, not established findings. They may be
commissioned separately or sequenced; retain the exact data and code needed to
reproduce each result. Pursue a supported remedy, not diagnosis alone.

A. Can the actual production fitter recover an exact, self-consistent answer?
   Generate sweep data from the same production geometry and parameterization
   used by its objective, including encoder-reference and coordinate-frame
   conventions. Verify the objective at generating anchors and check the
   analytic/autodiff gradients against independent finite differences over
   several step sizes. Fit both from the generating anchors and from realistic
   initial guesses without using truth to seed the operational experiment.
   Vary floating-point precision where supported, solver tolerances, stopping
   rules and conditioning; report objective residuals, convergence, per-anchor
   errors, sensitivity and failure cases. Include gauge-fixed known-radii and
   explicitly separate unknown-radii cases. If the exact self-consistent case
   fails, reduce it to a reproducible numerical, implementation or optimizer
   defect before attributing errors to physical modelling. An ideal static
   point-range calculation alone does not exercise the production pipeline.

B. Does the true geometry explain the recorded measurements?
   On preserved simulated sweeps, evaluate the same production objective at
   the true plant geometry and at the applied answer, using the correct
   reference, frame, radii and units. Break signed and absolute residuals down
   by sweep, point, role and force setting; distinguish individual candidates,
   final consensus and automatic stop. Assess whether a lower residual at an
   incorrect geometry indicates bias or weak conditioning, and whether the
   true geometry itself has substantial systematic residuals. Compare with an
   exact production-generated control and explicitly check filtering, invalid
   ellipses, weighting and numerical scaling. Truth is permitted only for this
   simulation diagnosis and scoring, never for measurement choice or the
   applied answer.

C. Why does zero-force projection fail in the tested world?
   First verify which ten force-trial measurements are actually retained; add
   a research-only trace if necessary. At selected repeatable measurement
   points, record commanded force and all encoder readings, together with
   simulated actual cable tensions, cable extension, motor deflection, mover
   position and settling/vibration over time. Compare the fitted linear
   intercept with the observed force-response curve and physical quantities;
   repeat force sequences in both directions and, where feasible, with matched
   positions and return-to-start controls. Test linearity, hysteresis, slack,
   motion during the force sequence and repeatability. Distinguish commanded
   motor force from measured cable tension: simulated truth is diagnostic, not
   an extra operational sensor. Fixed lengths are still one post-sweep scalar
   on the known-radius path, not per-point projected fixed lengths; measure
   that approximation separately. Do not equate role-swapped endpoint indices
   with the same physical position: the earlier reconstruction found they can
   occupy different branches. Use the corrected production projection handling
   and report settling failures as outcomes. Only develop an encoder-only
   correction when a falsifiable mechanism and independent validation support it.

D. Which physical effects cause the discrepancy, singly and together?
   Use matched, fresh worlds and verify each intervention reaches the baked
   engine. Hold measurement path and commanded forces fixed where feasible,
   so disabling noise does not silently change force tuning or the collected
   data. Starting from a declared control, vary injected encoder noise,
   quantization, cable elasticity, motor friction/compliance, vibration and
   settling one at a time where independently configurable, then test relevant
   combinations and interactions. Record actual settings, trajectories,
   model residuals at true and applied geometry, final per-anchor errors and
   failed workflows. Do not label an unsupported or unverified switch a
   negative result. Follow a diagnosed contribution with a feasible remedy,
   freeze its operational rules without truth, and evaluate on different
   friction settings and anchor layouts.

1. Which force and measurement choices avoid large errors across machines?
   The fixed-force study already sampled motor Coulomb/stiction variation and
   noisy encoder reads in three worlds; its two completed pairs are encouraging
   but too small to establish robustness. Investigate an observable adaptive
   force or measurement policy, or a specific model-bias discriminator, in
   different worlds. Keep guide friction zero until its transport/load model is validated.
   Extend to stiffness, layouts and payload when they resolve a specific gap.
   First verify each intervention changes the intended physical property in the
   baked native world. Distinguish unsupported parameters from negative results.
   Screen a small declared design, then reserve different worlds for evaluation.
   Compare current automatic force tuning with a materially different encoder-based policy. The fixed4mid rule above is settled for this matrix; reopen it only with a different observable rule, measured motor properties or a corrected model. If the transport fix is present, a fresh full-autocal run can test its integration, but cannot erase the prior timeout or prove its cause.
   Do not let simulated truth set per-world forces or select the winning rule.

2. Can force-varied measurements resolve solver disagreement?
   Freeze L-BFGS-B and TRF candidates from the same encoder prefix. Use their
   predicted measurements and observable noise to choose a feasible extra sweep,
   potentially at a different sensor force. Compare both frozen candidates on
   exactly the same fresh whole sweep before refitting. Compare with the existing
   planner and a matched extra-sweep control. Measure whether the decision also
   improves applied geometry, rather than assuming prediction cost proves it.
   If solvers agree but are both biased, can a force change expose model error?

3. Which missing measurement would constrain the weak geometry directions?
   Known radii supply a metric length-change reference; joint unknown radii and
   geometry introduce additional scale ambiguity. Distinguish gauge freedoms,
   weak numerical conditioning and systematic model bias. Use local Jacobian
   checks, controlled synthetic cases and fresh held-out sweeps to find which
   feasible encoder-only experiment adds independent information. Do not repeat
   the failed retrospective origin shift or add unidentifiable nuisance terms.

4. Can smaller calibration experiments isolate a useful model error first?
   For example, test bidirectional hysteresis or force-dependent apparent length
   at repeated encoder configurations before fitting geometry. Treat commanded
   forces as commands, not calibrated tension measurements. Test whether a
   correction transfers to unseen sweeps/worlds and improves actual anchors.
   The 2026-11-06 point-specific experiments measured deviations and tested
   two fitting approaches; neither established a reliable improvement. Use
   those results to distinguish a physical/model mechanism before revisiting.

Prioritize the scaling gauge, weak geometry directions and other barriers to
sub-millimetre accuracy. Distinguish exact ambiguity from weak conditioning and
model bias, and test ways to resolve each under explicitly stated measurement
assumptions. Record rival explanations and falsifying observations, then expand
promising approaches into independent worlds and fresh applied calibrations.
Do not require manual physical measurements. State any known nominal radius
assumption and do not substitute simulated truth for an operational measurement.
Do not abandon encoder-only calibration while plausible alternatives remain.

Execution and reporting

- Read research-program/roles/researcher.md, research/README.md and
  research/autocal-recording-performance.md. Use the local .venv/bin/python.
- Start with saved/synthetic evidence, then collect when the question requires
  fresh data. Use native Rust, supported --speedup 25, events for routine live
  work and none for fit-only matrices. Keep detailed recordings for motion
  questions. Run the supervisor research_preflight and a separate command-context
  scripts/research_socket_probe.py before long live collection.
- Declare finite worker/review time, fits, collections, points, physical steps,
  simulated time and cleanup budgets. Preserve unsuccessful and partial trials.
- Verify plant settings, encoder reference, coordinate frame, dataset lineage,
  source and native artifact hashes. Stock configure_session currently rejects
  custom anchor layouts; use an isolated documented research harness if needed.
- Report anchor RMSE with its precise definition, final applied error,
  per-iteration distribution/tail errors, invalid fits, automatic acceptance,
  points, sweeps, simulated seconds, steps and wall time. Separate selection-only,
  fit-only, synthetic, fresh simulated and hardware evidence. Do not pool repeated
  datasets as independent physical worlds.
- Freeze measurement/selection rules without test-world truth. State development
  versus evaluation worlds and every exception. No large-error reliability claim
  follows from a matrix containing no large errors.
- Implement only a supported change and run relevant autocal checks. Physics,
  motor or dynamics changes require npm run test:physics. The human authorized
  pushing supported, reviewed solutions;
  adopt production changes only when supported. Preserve one-click operation and automatic stopping.
- Maintain research.md, abstract.md and report.md with experiment IDs, measured
  outcomes, references, limitations and next decision. Regenerate the catalog
  with .venv/bin/python scripts/research_index.py. Research Programs use the
  controller's fixed submission, separate review and result-saving workflow.

Durability: preserve and push small datasets, results, source snapshots and failed
trial records after each completed experiment. End-of-turn saving alone is not
sufficient protection against workspace interruption. Keep large journals in a
separate durable artifact store and record any omission explicitly.
