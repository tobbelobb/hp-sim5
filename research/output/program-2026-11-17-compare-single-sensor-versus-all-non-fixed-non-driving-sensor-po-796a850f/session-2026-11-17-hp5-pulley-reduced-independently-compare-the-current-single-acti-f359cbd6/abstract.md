# HP5 pulley-reduced redundant-axis sensor policy

At production `forceLow` the compliant redundant axis is a stalled slack accumulator (0 encoder travel over 79–140 mm of motion); pretensioning it only during measurement makes it a valid range at unchanged sweep coverage and improves rough-budget anchor uncertainty about 9× (median 18.3 → 1.97 mm under matched 0.02 mm length perturbations), while raising the global idle force instead collapses coverage 4× and degrades the fit.
