# Physical experiment evidence

These are compact copies from session `attempt-c18177d871fb404895e32c03363c8a5e`, revision `9de0ac2ae01edf2a879c63e42ef269419d349cf0` plus the numerical-only evidence commit `d571ff9c`. Each run contains its automatic invocation, final or partial manifest, baked native motor readback, and deterministically compressed collected sweep dataset. The original session also retains recordings and journals; those large files are not copied here. Original source paths inside manifests refer to that session and machine. `SHA256.json` identifies archived files.

The matched 6301 stock and half-turn applied maximum errors were 8.103 and 4.118 mm. On shifted-layout 7301, stock and half-turn applied maxima were 4.697 and 4.286 mm, with one anchor worse under half-turn. The frozen experimental point filter instead worsened 7301 maximum error to 11.723 mm; its decision and actual firmware readback are included. No trial attained the every-anchor <1 mm target. The 4mid-plus-half-turn attempt is partial because a copied 80-fit session guard stopped it before application; its native manifest and `failure.json` are preserved. These are paired policy runs in two layouts, not independent repeats for every row.

`halfturn-sweep_data_collection.mjs` is the session-local collector copy, including references to an older worktree. The direct external imports and hash comparisons are recorded in `external-import-audit.json`; this file is not a self-contained installation. The frozen protocols document which choices preceded each run. None of these research variants is a validated product change.

The 100 mm spacing plus half-turn development run applied A/B/C/D errors 5.725/2.973/0.965/7.722 mm, worsening the half-turn maximum. After extending the fit ledger, the fresh 4mid-plus-half-turn retry applied 7.485/3.260/2.352/12.541 mm. The earlier interrupted 4mid attempt remains part of the evidence.

Without injected encoder noise, the 6302 stock run still applied maximum error 3.678 mm; automatic force tuning differed from the noisy baseline, and encoder quantization remained. The matched half-turn run timed out during its first bootstrap sub-sweep and applied nothing. Encoder C moved 44.09 degrees in the last 30 simulated seconds. Its failure and timeout audit are retained. The 8301 robust candidate was development-selected and its frozen protocol is archived; its evaluation was not complete at this commit.

The frozen soft-L1 candidate applied 3.262/1.061/1.781/4.874 mm on fresh 8301, compared with stock 3.069/5.343/5.577/1.245 mm; two anchors worsened. Both runs used identical recorded encoder means and steps. A zero-command projection attempt then timed out in its first sub-sweep with ongoing encoder movement and no application. Its trace statistics and frozen retry protocol are preserved.

The projection retry with a 120-second simulated settling deadline completed and applied A/B/C/D errors 8.839/9.272/16.337/10.519 mm, worse than stock. It used the explicit projected-length option and firmware readback is in the manifest. The earlier 30-second timeout remains preserved. The projection/noise-mean semantics correction is separate commit 818eb53f and needs independent review; this physical test does not validate its accuracy.
