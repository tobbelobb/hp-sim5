## Findings

1. **P2 — Probe approval disables reserve checks beyond its local interval and preload.** In candidate `autocal/control/behaviors/sweep_data_collection.mjs:1283`, the probe tests every endpoint at the single force `forceAt(toward)`. At line 1297, `reserveProbe.accepted` then disables the half-turn check for every measurement, without checking the measured drive position or force against that evidence. Profiles change force at line 1334. The [encoder reproduction](evidence/reserve-review.mjs), case `changedPreload` in its [output](evidence/reserve-review.json), accepts a reversible probe at 1.07854 N on drive interval −576.438 to −552.876 mm, then records the final sample at drive 0 mm and 3 N with **zero sensor rewind**. The mock has resolved, reversible coupling at the tested force and a stationary uncoupled response only at forces above 2.5 N. The otherwise identical `changedPreloadWithoutProbe` control rejects that final point. Keep approval local to the actually tested drive interval and preload, retain the strict reserve elsewhere, and invalidate or refresh evidence when force changes. The physical frequency of this constructed force-dependent failure is unknown; the missing enforcement is demonstrated.

2. **P2 — The fitted noise mean bypasses the first-sweeps half-turn check.** The raw settled sample is guarded at candidate `sweep_data_collection.mjs:1297`, but lines 1407–1424 append the subsequent noise mean and its `l_sensor_mu` without the reserve check. The default pose fitter prefers that mean (`autocal/pose_fit.py:30`). In case `meanCrossing`, the reserve and settled raw reading show **180.010°** rewind. A **0.1°** encoder shift immediately after the quiet check makes the 16-sample mean show **179.91625°**, which is still published through `onPoint` and returned as calibration data. Its unchanged-encoder control passes with every mean inside reserve. Validate the actual values admitted to fitting before publishing the point, including raw and mean data. A quiet check before sampling does not establish that all later samples remain in reserve.

3. **P2 — Satisfying reserve can consume the entire measured interval and produce a zero-arc sweep.** Candidate `sweep_data_collection.mjs:1268` can place `measurementStartMm` exactly at `driveStartPointMm`. When rewind succeeds there, no remaining-interval check runs before line 1328 computes `stepDelta = 0`. Case `consumedInterval` uses an observed 20 mm drive excursion and reversible sensor gain 10: reserve reaches 200°, and the collector returns **three identical measurements at drive 0 mm**. It reports the original −20 to 0 mm drive range although no measurement arc remains. The 20 mm/gain-4 control correctly rejects insufficient rewind. Check the interval remaining after reserve selection and any accepted probe, and reject a direction without a measurable arc. Otherwise these nonempty sweeps can also be treated as completed frontier evidence by the original `workspace.py` policy.

The parent was informed of these three cases and accepted them for correction. This report does not certify the later corrections.

## Workspace and remaining restrictions

The new `exploration_grid` takes only the dataset and requested grid count. A positive size-tuned excursion supplies the step; completed fixed targets extend each anchor's observed range by another step. Empty sweeps do not extend it. Both rough and main default HP3/HP4 planners use it. The L1 fixed-target budget and lower-anchor nonnegative bounds are removed, while carrying D remains fixed with D ≤ 0. Physical bounds are intersected with the exploration bounds. Explicit user candidate ranges retain their explicit behavior.

The [analytical comparison](evidence/workspace-review.py) uses the existing unit-test tetrahedron only as a review fixture. Production receives none of those coordinates. With a 390 mm step and nine nominal grid values, the maximum sampled Z at the unchanged 0.25 pretension share rises from approximately **550 → 848 → 1204 mm** after synthetic completed D probes at **0 → −390 → −780 mm**. These are geometric predictions across all generated candidates, not actual collection results or a guarantee that the information ranker chooses the frontier.

The unchanged self-stress arc heuristic remains a material restriction. After a completed D = −1170 mm probe, the next D bound is −1560 mm, but the 0.25 sampler reaches only approximately **1283 mm** in this fixture; its 0.05 counterpart reaches approximately **1599 mm**. Fixed A = +780 mm and D = −1560 mm yields zero ranking poses at 0.25, while the existing 0.05 force-profile path yields **44 positive-tension poses** around Z = 1567–1584 mm under the stated synthetic 1 N load and 1 N strand target. Their force-balance residual is below 2e−15 N. [Exact inputs and results](evidence/workspace-review.json) preserve these assumptions.

That is evidence of conservative model censorship, not physical permission to visit those poses. Positive static tension does not establish servo reachability, safe spool state, actuator limits, compliance, or actual force tracking. The sampler also chooses the reachable stretch nearest home, uses 360 angular samples, and rejects stretches shorter than the requested point count. These restrictions can still suppress useful outer arcs. Wider numerical bounds therefore need achieved-coverage evidence; the appropriate next comparison is predicted versus measured trajectories and useful pose/force diversity. Default spread stopping is now disabled, but existing patience stopping remains, with no new achieved-coverage criterion.
