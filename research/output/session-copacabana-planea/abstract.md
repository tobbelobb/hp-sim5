# COPacabana PlaneA replay

With live moving swivel pulleys and kinematic encoder replay (`3.55e-15 rad` tracking error), the simulator settles within 1.5 mm of the controller's commanded pose at every PlaneA stop. The real platform misses that pose by up to 20.6 mm because its Y travel is 3.5–4.6 % shorter than `COPacabana.xml` predicts. That hardware geometry mismatch over-constrains the cables, and the logged `f[i]` readings also carry a `1/√2` carriage-pulley scale factor plus upper/lower guide-pulley friction (~13 % vs ~4 %), explaining both the 183 N force RMS and the apparent static-equilibrium residual.
