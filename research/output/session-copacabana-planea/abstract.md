# COPacabana PlaneA replay

With live moving swivel pulleys, kinematic encoder replay (`3.55e-15 rad` max error), bottom-rail reflector placement (`z = 0.0225 m`), and explicit `√2` sensor scaling on P0 initial tensions, the simulator settles within 2.1 mm of the controller's commanded pose (15.8 mm RMS vs tracker). The real platform misses that pose by up to 20.6 mm along Y (`3.5–5.7 %` Y-travel shortfall on PlaneA and CircPath while X and Z travel `0.5–0.9 %` long). Constrained ablation shows that elasticity, sensor scaling, and guide friction alone cannot close the gap without geometric/homing corrections, while a `1/√2` carriage-pulley sensor factor (shared across DaRUS-3600/5171/4614) plus upper/lower guide friction (~13 % vs ~4 %) explains the apparent static-equilibrium failure. Published `COPacabana.xml` geometry and transmission are preserved untouched as the baseline. [Report](report.md)
