# Uneven low-anchor heights: free every low z, fix the gauge at the top anchor

[Simulation evidence](report.md): the old equal-height fit turned one low anchor that is h mm off into 0.68·h of anchor error on HP3/HP4, as an exact, undetectable tilt gauge, and 2–2.4·h on HP5/HP5r, where it was a real wrong constraint and broke a live HP5 run at h = 5 mm. Fitting every low z, with the gauge fixed by top x = y = 0 and A.x = 0 (now the code's convention), keeps error flat in h (HP3 1.0, HP4 0.7, HP5 1.0, HP5r 1.6 mm for |h| ≤ 20 mm), costs ≤ 0.3 mm at equal heights and leaves the D.z/stiffness coupling unchanged. On HP5 the free gauge made the sweep jackknife print "poor" for sub-mm calibrations, because one fold lost a coordinate's only constraint. The jackknife now leaves such a fold out of that coordinate alone (detected from pose information) and reports the coordinate as single-sweep supported. False "poor" fell from 4/9 to 1/9, with no loss on 56 single-sweep corruptions and no corpus decision change; a median spread was rejected for hiding single-sweep failures.
