# Compliance observability: a fixed-pose force step measures line compliance to ±4%

[Simulation evidence](report.md): the stiffness→D.z trade is large only on HP5 pulley-reduced sweeps (8.4 mm per unit compliance) and in the ellipsoid shape (5–10 mm); on HP3 the ~1 mm common z shift is ABC home stretch, and a ~150 s force-step probe recovers the true-compliance correction within 0.04 mm of D.z on nine live HP3 runs at EA ×0.8–1.2.
