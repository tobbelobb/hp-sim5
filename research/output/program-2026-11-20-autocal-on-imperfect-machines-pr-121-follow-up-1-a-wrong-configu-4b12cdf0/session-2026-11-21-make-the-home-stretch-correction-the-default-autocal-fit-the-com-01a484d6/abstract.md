# Home-stretch correction is now the default on HP3 (already on HP4; built into HP5/HP5r's stretch model)

[Simulation evidence](report.md): live, paired on 10 imperfect HP3 seeds, the ABC home-strain term cuts the print-frame max error from 1.06 ± 0.31 to 0.44 ± 0.10 mm (better in 10/10, nominal 1.19 → 0.60, same stops); HP4/HP5/HP5r are byte-identical, and forcing the term onto HP5/HP5r would double-count their per-point stretch (max error ×2).
