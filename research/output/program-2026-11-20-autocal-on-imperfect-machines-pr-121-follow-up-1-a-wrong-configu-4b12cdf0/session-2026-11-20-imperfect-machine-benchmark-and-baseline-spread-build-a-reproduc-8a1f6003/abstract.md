# Imperfect-machine autocal benchmark: HP3 holds about 1.1 mm in the print frame; uneven low heights are a gauge on three low anchors

[Simulation evidence](report.md): on 10 random HP3 machines (low anchors ±30 mm, top anchor off-centre ≤50 mm, EA ±20%), the default autocal leaves 1.11 ± 0.35 mm max error in the print frame. Most of it is the known common z shift; stiffness is the only perturbation with a measurable (sub-0.25 mm) effect. Raw scene-frame errors of 8–28 mm are an exact rigid tilt that length data cannot see. HP5 with four uneven low anchors fails the shared-z fit (4–19 mm, flagged by rms/jackknife). A force auto-tune knife-edge failed 1/10 machines and is fixed. Freeing the low z values (`a7a8d179`) is equivalent on HP3 (−0.06 ± 0.03 mm, paired) and fixes both HP5 machines (0.52 and 1.00 mm at nominal time).
Follow-up: the harness now covers HP4 and HP5r (unperturbed runs reproduce CI), and on the free-low-z fit 20/20 random machines complete (HP3 1.06 ± 0.31, HP4 0.53 ± 0.04, HP5 0.95 ± 0.34, HP5r 1.23 ± 0.04 mm in the print frame).
