# Firmware spool flattening and strained storage

Modelling drum flattening and strained rope storage in RRF and Klipper flex compensation raises the closed-loop headless Print Squares score on HP3, HP4, HP5 and HP5r with both firmwares (for example HP4 RRF 85.9 → 95.2, HP5r Klipper 90.7 → 96.3). [Report](report.md)
