# HP4 autocal D.z bias: effector roll and slack parallel lines

[Simulation evidence](report.md): chiral D reeving on one shared spool makes the HP4 effector roll about 0.2°/m off-centre, which slackens one of each ABC parallel line pair and biases D.z by about 5 mm. Stiffness scaling cannot fix this. Keeping the per-strand target independent of mechanical advantage cuts HP4 error from 4.57 to 1.52 mm and HP5 pulley-reduced from 2.57 to 1.34 mm.
