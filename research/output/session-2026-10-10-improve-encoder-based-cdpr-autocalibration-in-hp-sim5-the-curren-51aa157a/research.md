Initial ideas (not tested):
1. Redundant cable-length consistency with unknown per-point poses (joint geometric fit or pose elimination), respecting coordinate gauges.
2. Use measured fixed-motor encoder deviations instead of pretending all fixed lengths are exact.
3. Select whole held-out sweeps and assess geometry observability; do not reward history age/coverage independently of prediction accuracy.
4. Collect broad, informative sparse sweeps; use parameter-sensitive endpoints and intermediate points rather than repeated low-information samples.
5. Profile force trials, positioning, settling, encoder averaging, returns and optimization separately; shorten only dominant validated waits.
6. Detect unobservable directions and use uncertainty/stability to stop automatically with an honest quality label.
