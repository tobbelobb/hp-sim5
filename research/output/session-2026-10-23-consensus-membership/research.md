# Finite-prediction consensus beyond the motivating screen

Objective: decide whether finite-held-out-prediction membership should replace
all-candidate final consensus, for more useful encoder-only calibration from the
same observations. Task 004 authorizes implementation only if evidence supports
adoption. Production defaults remain unchanged during this investigation.

H1: excluding unvalidated candidates improves applied anchor accuracy without
worsening any additional saved dataset. H2: finite predictions of individual
members suffice to prevent invalid/worse predictions by the applied average and
the best-ranked candidate's spool model. Neither is established by the initial
three-history screen.

Evidence first: reuse the initial screen without rerunning it. Read the October
15 spread-gate report, its eleven-history inventory and verification script,
current history selection, predictive validation, stage decoding and consumers.
Eight dataset names are additional to the motivating three. The three overlaps
are revision sensitivity cases, not independent confirmation. Dataset variation
does not imply independent physical worlds; all evidence is simulated.

Predeclared probe budget (2026-10-23): at most eleven saved histories, twenty-two
final selections and fifty-five frozen prediction evaluations, within fifteen
minutes of numerical work. Zero fresh fits, physics runs/steps, collections or
movement; no recorder or Viewer. Queue ceiling remains 45 minutes total. Use
the existing 0.01 mm merge and 5 mm spread thresholds, saved single-fit ordering
and actual chosen spool parameters. No rule/threshold search. Truth measures
accuracy only. A worsening additional dataset rejects unconditional adoption;
preserve exceptions and assess whether any narrower scope is defensible.

Archive compressed original envelopes with hashes and dataset truth for portable
reproduction. Decode with read_stage_artifact, confirm candidates agree with
their saved evaluated entries, preserve their arrays, and record source drift.
Recompute each chosen single fit's saved future-fold prediction once to expose
relevant scoring incompatibility. Score each applied arm on that same fold with
its actual spool model/settings; mark any member's training overlap explicitly.
Also score on a common wholly unseen fold when one exists after excluding the
training sweeps of every applied member and the chosen spool fit in both arms.
No overlap-contaminated score is evidence of held-out generalization.

Consumer finding: autocal.py calls select_history_plan for final summaries and
acceptance. stopping.py calls consensus_anchor_estimate on raw candidates.
A change restricted to select_history_plan would leave stopping/fitting and
collection intact; changing the shared consensus helper would need a trajectory
replay. This experiment tests only frozen final selection.

Next decision: run the one predeclared probe, inspect failures and coverage,
then adopt/narrow/retire with an explicit scope. No experiment result yet.

CM-E1 completed: the one probe used eleven histories, 22 selections and 49
prediction evaluations (2.259 s timed section, imports excluded). On eight
additional dataset names, one improved, two worsened and five were unchanged:
401.100276 -> 400.797031 mm summed anchor error. Regressions were flexible lines
(+0.502998 mm) and ten-points buildup (+0.094156 mm); buildup2 improved by
0.900399 mm. The three overlapping names were unchanged in this older corpus.
All eleven recomputed chosen single-fit predictions match saved scores. Every
applied-model diagnostic is finite, but every changed comparison lacks a common
unseen sweep; the chosen-fit future folds overlap consensus training in both
arms. This does not establish predictive improvement. No unsuccessful trial
was removed and no rule/threshold was retuned.

The separate nonnumerical audit passed: eleven distinct observation hashes,
input/script identities match results, and candidate errors/training coverage
are saved in audit.json. Original envelopes are compressed beside the result;
the probe does not need the producer worktree. This is a task-specific evidence
copy, not the general handoff/status workflow requested by task 006.

Decision: H1's nonworsening claim is rejected; H2's predictive-safety claim is
inconclusive. Retire the blanket default-change proposal and preserve production
policy. No narrower deployable rule has been tested. Reopening this route needs
a truth-free discriminator evaluated on common unseen sweeps for the regression
cases, searching saved later sweeps before proposing fits or collection. Task
006 remains next in queue order; task 005 should consider this negative result.

Actual fits/physics/collections/movement: zero. No runtime source changed; the
mandatory physics delivery gate does not apply. Catalog/link/format checks are
