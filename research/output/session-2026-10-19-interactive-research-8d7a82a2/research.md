## H5 / E08 proposed counterfactual
Source algebra reveals a narrower candidate than removing stabilization: after the already-integrated rotor pose is used to compute cable load, the velocity update omits the K*dt*omega prediction correction. It therefore has a steady extra torque -K*dt*omega. Add that prediction term in an isolated native artifact, retaining the full implicit denominator. This makes delta-omega = dt*(drive+current cable load)/(I+dt*D+dt^2*K), preserving steady torque balance while damping acceleration. NOT tested and NOT installed in production. Build and test only the archived native engine copy before deciding whether to change source. Reeving maps this artificial rotor drag through U^2/r^2: horizontal HP4 U=2 versus HP3 U=1 gives 4x reflected drag for equal effective force.
