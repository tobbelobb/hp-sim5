Keep nominal known radii, solver, stop and force policies unchanged. Run a fresh 6301 development world with the same finite step and wall limits, after the 4mid trial. Compare chosen target separation, count-adjusted production-objective information, automatic final applied errors and terminal outcomes against stock and half-turn.
