# Soft-L1 point residual candidate

This candidate was developed retrospectively on 5301, 6301 and 7301, including a range of soft-L1 scales; none of those is held out for it. At normal automatic acceptance, starting at the stock selected anchors, refit all production pointwise signed approximate-mm residuals with SciPy least_squares soft_l1 loss and f_scale 0.03 mm. This fixed transition is consistent with about 0.06 degree single-reading encoder noise at the known nominal 30 mm radius; it is an operational noise-scale assumption, not a plant-truth input, an anchor measurement or a guarantee. It was also development-selected, so evaluate only on new worlds. Keep sample collection and normal stopping unchanged. Check all sweeps yield stable finite residuals and the fit succeeds; if invalid, if the residual vector changes length, or if any anchor moves more than 50 mm relative to the stock selection, apply stock as fallback and log the reason. The candidate must explicitly disclose that the stock displayed quality score does not evaluate the replacement. Record actual M669 sent and firmware readback. Never consult plant truth to fit, trigger, fallback or choose the answer.

On a fresh 8301 layout with changed motor friction and new noise seed, run stock and robust policies sequentially in independently initialized matched worlds, with 8M-step and 2400-second job caps. Verify baked anchor and motor properties before post-hoc per-anchor scoring. No tuning after seeing 8301. This tests one new layout/friction setting and does not prove reliability or <1 mm accuracy, even if it improves.
