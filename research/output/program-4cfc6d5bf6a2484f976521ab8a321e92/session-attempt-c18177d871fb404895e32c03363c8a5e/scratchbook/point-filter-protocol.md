# Frozen point-filter candidate, fresh layout 7301

After normal stock full-auto acceptance, start each leave-one-sweep unfiltered least-squares fit at the selected automatic anchors. For each held-out sweep, compute its RMS in the production pointwise approximate-mm residual and divide by the median RMS of the other sweeps. Choose the largest ratio. Trigger only when ratio exceeds 10 and held-out RMS exceeds 0.3 mm. Reject points on that sweep whose absolute residual exceeds the greater of 0.3 mm or ten times the median absolute residual in the remaining sweeps. Require at least 10 points remain in that sweep. Refit the resulting dataset unfiltered, starting from the selected automatic anchors. Apply the refit only when the solver succeeds, its anchors are finite and each differs from stock by at most 50 mm. Otherwise apply stock. This candidate is encoder-only with known nominal 30 mm radii, no plant truth and no manual measurement. The 10 and 0.3 cutoffs were developed on 5301 and 6301 and may not generalize. Record the trigger, selected sweep, point indices, values and final command; never pretend the stock quality score certifies the replacement. This rule handles at most one inconsistent sweep and cannot establish accuracy.
