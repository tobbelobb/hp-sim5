## Implemented behavior

ProgramManager exposes route_plan, commission, strategy_result, route_decide and
strategy through its retained CLI/MCP host. overview writes a cited brief and
returns the durable board and next allocation. The route records preserve
hypotheses, practical tradeoffs, objections, cost, novelty, uncertainty, decision
value, dependency citations and reopening conditions. Append order chooses the
latest plan, and restore preserves that order.

Commission records at least two rival explanations and observation-dependent
actions before creating a ready bounded assignment. Scouting, literature,
tooling and experiments all dispatch through the existing worker. The worker
receives the actual investigation and accepted human direction. Runtime
allocations require positive finite wall time and finite nonnegative explicit
counters. Tests fund two to four fits and a collection where appropriate;
zero counters belong to named saved-source examples. Collection guidance retains
speedup 25 and event recording. This task adds no runtime deadline service.

Automatic scientific results require a current keep admission for the same
assignment and matching finding. A review-ID citation reaches the fixed
submission, admission, claim scope and selected artifact sources. Blocked,
unrelated, invalidated and withdrawn findings fail atomically. Separately
reviewed partial findings from a failed worker remain usable. A surprising
supported result can promote its rival, since surprise is separate from the
review finding. A tool failure changes no research state and consumes no
scientific retry. Reviewed inconclusive results reach a recorded limit, park
the route and require reframing before repeated funding. An older narrow
outcome cannot retire a route after its hypothesis, scope or observation changes.

Graph withdrawals, applicability changes, invalidated submissions and later
withheld reviews trigger dependency review. Surviving derivations retain route
state while funding waits for an explicit cited review. Loss of a required
support parks a live/promising route. Notes and hunches retain provenance;
accepted instructions select or replace a pending allocation while unrelated
running work continues. The old pending contract and all earlier evidence stay
recorded.
