Leading hypotheses after source reading:
- H1: history validation uses the latest candidate's modeled dataset for all candidates, so radius/buildup errors are not scored for the candidate actually selected.
- H2: candidate-specific future folds are incomparable and exclude the latest estimate; a common unseen sweep could improve ranking fairness.
- H3: training filters/noise normalization can prefer physically worse geometry; unfiltered robust held-out encoder residuals can reject these updates.
- H4: bounded residual-driven updates can improve convergence, but radius/scale/buildup ambiguity may defeat local adjustment.
