# Early core ideas

- Give the manager a continuing research question and human priorities.
- Reuse the existing research agent and simulation tools.
- Let the researcher investigate one useful question at a time.
- Choose the next direction using earlier results, including failed ideas.
- Keep productive investigations in their existing conversations.
- Ask a separate reviewer to check useful results and proposed code.
- Preserve measurements and link claims to the evidence that supports them.
- Keep research findings separate from whether code is ready to use.
- Respect human steering and stop requests.
- Deliver tested improvements and a short account of what changed.
- Add management machinery only when its benefit justifies its cost.

Source: the historical [design proposal](archive/research-program-design.md).
